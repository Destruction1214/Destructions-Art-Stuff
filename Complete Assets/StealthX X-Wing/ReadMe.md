Description:
Nigh pure black X-Wing, the 'StealthX' used by Lukes New Jedi Order and entered service around the end of the Yuuzhan Vong war.
It was heavily stripped down of technology, to become near undetectable to sensor systems while its black paint made it basically invisible to the eye.
The pilots relied on the force, as it was specifically designed to be used by the Jedi.
